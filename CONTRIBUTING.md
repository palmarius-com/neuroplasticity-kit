# Contributing

The kit grows by use. What you learn while using it is welcome here.

## Whose Work This Is

The kit is the private work of Collin Sale, written in his spare time. He
is the rights holder. Palmarius GmbH may use and publish it.

## How

Open an **issue**. Pull requests are not accepted.

The reason: the kit has one rights holder, so that the licence stays clear
and a licence without the credit duty can be granted (see `NOTICE.md`).
Text merged from many authors would end that. An issue lets you say what
you found; the wording that goes into the kit is then written here.

## What Helps

| What | How to send it |
|---|---|
| A rule that proved itself in your project | The entry from your `_session/this_repo/kit_proposals.md`, with the situation it came from. No names, no customer detail |
| A rule of the kit that did harm or was misread | Which rule, what the assistant did, what you expected |
| An acceptance test result | Model and version, kit version, the five rows of `procedures/acceptance_test.md` with pass or fail |
| A hook configuration for another assistant | The configuration and the product version it worked with |
| A translation of the two surveys | The questions and options in your language |
| A bug in a script | The command, the output, your PowerShell version |

Leave out anything confidential. An issue is public.

## What Happens to It

By opening an issue you agree that what you write there may be used in the
kit, in any wording, under the kit's licence and under any other licence
the rights holder grants, without a claim on your side. If you do not want
that, do not send it.

You are named in the kit's change notes if you wish; say so in the issue.

## Other Ways to Give Back

- Keep the credit line and the link where you use the kit. The licence
  asks for it; it is also what keeps the kit alive.
- Tell others how it worked for you.
- Setup, migration of existing repositories and training are offered as a
  service: contact in `NOTICE.md`.
